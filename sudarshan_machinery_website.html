What We Supply

Comprehensive range of industrial machinery for manufacturing, fabrication, and process industries.

CNC Machining Centers

Vertical and horizontal CNC machining centers from 3-axis to 5-axis. Fanuc, Siemens, and Mitsubishi controls available.

Hydraulic Press Systems

H-frame and C-frame hydraulic presses from 10T to 500T. Suitable for forging, stamping, and forming applications.

Material Handling Systems
